# Disclaimer

Dharmabot Legal Skills are instructions for AI coding and agent tools. They are not legal advice and they do not create an advocate–client relationship.

- **Drafts only.** Every output is a draft for review by an advocate enrolled with a State Bar Council in India. Court filings, appearances and advice remain the responsibility of the advocate who uses them.
- **Law changes.** Statutes, rules, notifications, court-fee and stamp schedules, and forms change. Each skill tells the AI to retrieve the current official text before relying on any provision, and to mark anything it could not verify. Each skill records a `law_as_of` date in `skill-registry.yaml`.
- **No guarantees.** Outputs may be wrong, incomplete or out of date. The licence (MIT) disclaims all warranties.
- **Confidentiality.** Before giving client documents to any AI tool, make sure the tool, its model provider and its settings are consistent with your duties of confidentiality, the Bar Council of India Rules and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Remove or pseudonymise client identifiers where you can.
- **No advertising.** This project does not solicit work for any advocate or firm, in keeping with Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules.
