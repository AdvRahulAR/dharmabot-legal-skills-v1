# Startup fundraise

**When to use:** an Indian startup is raising a priced equity round or a convertible round from Indian or foreign investors.
**Who it is for:** startup lawyers acting for the company or founders (investor-side counsel can follow the same chain).

## Steps

1. `client-intake-conflict-screener` and `engagement-letter-drafter`. Output: intake note, conflict result and engagement letter.
   **STOP:** advocate clears the conflict (especially if also acting for an investor) and the client signs.
2. `startup-legal-health-checker`. Input: incorporation documents, cap table, IP assignments, filings. Output: gaps to fix before diligence.
3. `vc-term-sheet-reviewer`. Input: term sheet. Output: economics and control analysis with suggested changes.
4. `convertible-instrument-analyst` if CCPS, CCDs, iSAFE or notes are involved. Output: instrument structure and conversion mechanics.
5. `cap-table-calculator`. Input: term sheet, current cap table, ESOP pool. Output: pre- and post-money table, dilution and waterfall.
   **STOP:** founders approve the term sheet positions and the cap table.
6. `india-entry-fema-structuring-analyst` if any investor is non-resident. Output: route, sectoral caps, pricing rules and filings needed.
7. `shareholders-agreement-drafter`. Input: signed term sheet. Output: SHA and SSA drafts.
   **STOP:** advocate approves the drafts before they go to investor counsel.
8. `contract-negotiation-planner` and `adversarial-draft-reviewer` for each round of mark-ups.
9. `esop-scheme-drafter` if the pool is created or expanded. Output: scheme and approvals.
10. `securities-issuance-compliance-checker`. Input: issue structure. Output: private placement or preferential allotment steps, valuation and offer documents.
11. `board-meeting-documents-drafter`. Output: board and shareholder resolutions, notices and minutes, and articles amendments.
12. `closing-checklist-builder`. Output: conditions precedent, closing deliverables and post-closing actions.
    **STOP:** advocate confirms all conditions precedent are met before funds are released.
13. `contract-execution-stamping-checker` and `state-stamp-duty-calculator`. Output: stamping of SHA/SSA and duty on share issue.
14. After closing: `roc-secretarial-compliance-checker` for the allotment filings; `fema-regularisation-drafter` if any FEMA reporting is late.
15. `compliance-calendar-builder`. Output: post-closing filings and recurring investor reporting.

## Deadlines to compute with `limitation-calculator`

- Allotment after receipt of money, and the ROC return of allotment.
- FEMA reporting of foreign inward remittance and share issue.
- Stamp duty payment. Enter all dates in `matter-deadline-tracker`.

## Before filing

16. `pre-filing-verification-checker`. Input: allotment filings, FEMA forms and resolutions. Output: signed verification log. **STOP:** advocate signs, then files.
