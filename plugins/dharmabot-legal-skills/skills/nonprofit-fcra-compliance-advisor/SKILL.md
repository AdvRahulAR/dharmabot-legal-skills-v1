# Nonprofit and FCRA Compliance Advisor

Every output is a draft for an advocate enrolled in India and the organisation's chartered accountant to check.

## Purpose

Set out the right legal form, the registrations needed, and the steps and conditions for receiving and using foreign contribution lawfully.

## Jurisdiction gate

- Identify the State or UT of registration. Trust and society law differs by State; some States have public trust Acts and charity commissioners, others do not. Open `references/india-state-statutes.md` as a starting point.
- Confirm the donor's country and whether it is a "foreign source" under FCRA. Foreign donor-side tax points are marked "to be confirmed by counsel qualified in [jurisdiction]".

## Temporal gate

- The Income-tax Act, 2025 was reported to apply from 1 April 2026, renumbering the regime for charitable registration and donor deductions (formerly sections 12A or 12AB and 80G). Use the provisions for the relevant tax year and confirm.
- FCRA was amended in 2020 (for example limits on administrative expenses, no transfer to other persons, the SBI New Delhi main branch account). Check for any later amendment or rule change.

## Required inputs

**Blocking**:
- the organisation's legal form and registration documents, or the proposed form;
- its objects and actual activities, and how long it has existed with what spending;
- the proposed foreign donor, amount and purpose;
- details of trustees, members or directors, including nationality and any past FCRA or criminal issues.

Also useful: audited accounts, existing tax registrations, and grant agreement drafts.

## Method

1. Retrieve the current official text of the FCRA, 2010, the FCR Rules and MHA notices, the Income-tax Act provisions for charitable institutions, the Companies Act provisions on Section 8 companies, and the State trust or society law. Record version and date.
2. Choose or confirm the legal form: compare control, governance, State oversight and cost.
3. Tax: provisional and regular registration for exemption, approval for donor deductions, and timelines for applying. Note conditions on application of income and accumulation.
4. CSR-1 registration if the organisation will receive CSR funds.
5. FCRA route: registration (needs a track record; confirm the current eligibility criteria) or prior permission for a specific grant from a specific donor.
6. Account: foreign contribution must first be received in the designated FCRA account believed to be at the SBI New Delhi main branch (confirm against the current Act, rules and MHA notices [verify]); utilisation accounts may be opened separately.
7. Utilisation: the administrative expense cap, the bar on transferring to other persons, no use for prohibited purposes, and separate books.
8. Annual returns, intimation of changes in office bearers, and renewal timelines.
9. Red flags that lead to refusal or cancellation: political links, adverse reports, unrelated activities, or diversion. Flag them clearly.

## Output

1. Recommended structure with reasons.
2. Registration roadmap: step · authority · form · documents · time.
3. FCRA pathway and compliance checklist.
4. Risk note on refusal triggers.

## Guardrails and hand-offs

- Never suggest routing foreign money through an Indian intermediary to avoid FCRA.
- Never guarantee registration or approval.
- CSR questions from the donor side go to `csr-esg-brsr-compliance-checker`.
