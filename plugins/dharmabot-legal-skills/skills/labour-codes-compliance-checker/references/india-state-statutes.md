## How to use this table

1. Find the State or UT and note the statute named.
2. Retrieve the current official text of that statute, its Schedules, and every amendment and rate notification, from India Code, the State gazette or the State law department. Record the date.
3. If the row says "unconfirmed", or the official text differs from the table, rely on the official text and tell the user the table needs correcting. Contributions to fix rows are welcome.
4. Several States have passed tenancy laws based on the Model Tenancy Act, 2021 that come into force only on notification. Always check whether the new law has commenced and what happens to cases pending under the old one.
