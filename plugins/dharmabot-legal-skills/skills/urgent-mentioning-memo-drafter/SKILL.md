# Urgent Mentioning Memo Drafter

Every output is a draft for an advocate enrolled in India to check and sign.

## Purpose

Get the bench to understand in one page why the matter cannot wait, with accurate facts and no exaggeration.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm the forum is the Supreme Court or an Indian High Court (and which bench). Each court sets its own urgent-listing and mentioning procedure, and vacation practice changes each year. If the matter is in a district court, say that local practice applies and draft a plain urgency application.

## Required inputs

Mark as **blocking**:
- the court, bench and whether a regular, vacation or after-hours bench is sitting;
- the diary or case number, or confirmation that the petition is ready to file;
- the impugned action, its date, and the exact date and time of the threatened harm;
- prior proceedings in any forum and their outcome;
- whether the other side has lodged a caveat, and whether notice was given.

## Method

1. Retrieve the court's current official procedure for urgent listing, mentioning and vacation benches (circulars, notices, the urgency proforma). Record the source and date.
2. Test the urgency. Ask: when did the cause arise, when did the client act, and is any delay explained? Flag self-created urgency plainly to the advocate.
3. Draft the urgency letter or proforma in the court's format: case details, nature of urgency in two or three sentences, the irreparable harm, the date of the threatened step, interim relief sought, prior proceedings, and caveat status.
4. Draft a mentioning memo for oral mentioning where the court allows it, with a short oral script of under one minute.
5. Attach the documents that prove urgency (notice, order, demolition schedule).
6. If a caveat exists, prepare proof of service of the advance copy on the caveator.
7. Note the fallback if listing is refused: an alternate bench, the next day, or the registrar.

## Output

1. Urgency letter or proforma.
2. Mentioning memo and short oral script.
3. A checklist of urgency documents and caveat service.
4. A note to `hearing-day-brief-builder` for the listing day.

## Guardrails and hand-offs

- Never overstate urgency or invent a date or threat. Say so if urgency is weak or self-created.
- Do not draft the main petition or interim application here.
- Registry follow-up goes to `registry-letter-drafter`.
- The advocate mentions the matter; this skill only prepares the papers.
- Before anything is filed or sent, hand off to `pre-filing-verification-checker`. The Supreme Court has said that an advocate who cites AI-generated judgments without verifying them commits misconduct (Pooja Ramesh Singh v. Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd., 2026 INSC 668, decided 2 July 2026; read the judgment before citing it).
