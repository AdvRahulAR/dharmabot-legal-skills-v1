# Cross Examination Planner

Every output is a draft plan for an advocate enrolled in India to check and conduct.

## Purpose

Give the cross-examining advocate a short, disciplined plan: what to get from this witness, how, with which documents, and what to avoid.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm the trial is before an Indian court or tribunal and note the forum and State. For criminal trials, note that different rules on the accused and prosecution witnesses apply and confirm the plan fits the proceeding.

## Temporal gate

Check whether the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 or the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 governs this proceeding (the BSA applies from 1 July 2024; confirm the position for matters pending then) and cite the correct provisions on leading questions, contradiction by earlier statements and impeaching credit.

## Required inputs

Mark as **blocking**:
- the pleadings and issues framed;
- the witness's affidavit of evidence or prior testimony;
- the documents on record, including the other side's exhibits;
- the client's case on each issue.

Also useful: the witness's earlier statements (letters, emails, notices, reports), and the judge's known practice on time.

## Method

1. Retrieve the current official text of the evidence provisions on cross-examination, leading questions, previous statements in writing, questions affecting credit and indecent or scandalous questions, plus Order XVIII CPC. Record the version and date.
2. List the issues and decide, for each, what this witness can concede that helps, and what parts of the testimony must be challenged.
3. Build a contradiction table: witness's statement · earlier statement or document · page reference · the contradiction.
4. Draft short, closed, leading questions in a logical sequence for each objective. One fact per question.
5. Mark where a document will be put to the witness, and the foundation needed before it is shown.
6. Put the client's case to the witness on each material point, so it cannot later be said it went unchallenged.
7. Assess the risk of each line: what an unhelpful answer does to the case, and whether to skip it.
8. Prepare short re-examination risk notes for the other side.

## Output

1. An issue-wise plan with objectives, questions and documents.
2. The contradiction table with page references.
3. A "do not ask" list and the case to be put.

## Guardrails and hand-offs

- No scandalous, insulting or humiliating questions; no questions intended only to annoy.
- Never suggest misstating a document or the record to a witness.
- Do not invent prior statements or page references.
- Chronology support: `litigation-chronology-builder`. Our own witness: `evidence-affidavit-drafter`.
