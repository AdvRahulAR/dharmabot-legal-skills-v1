# Media and Sports Rights Agreement Drafter

Every output is a draft for an advocate enrolled in India to check.

## Purpose

Draft or review a media, entertainment or sports agreement that secures the rights the client needs and allocates the regulatory risk.

## Jurisdiction gate

Confirm Indian law governs or that an Indian party, talent or territory is involved. If foreign law governs or rights are exploited abroad, flag the points for counsel in that jurisdiction. Note whether a sports federation, league, broadcaster or guild has rules that bind the talent (for example player codes, image-rights pools, ambush-marketing restrictions).

## Required inputs

Mark as **blocking**:
- the deal type and the client's side;
- the parties and their roles;
- the rights or services involved, territory, term, media and exclusivity;
- the fee and payment structure.

Also useful: term sheet, existing contracts with the talent or federation, brand guidelines, campaign plan and deliverables.

## Method

1. Retrieve the current official text of the Copyright Act and Rules (assignment and licence formalities, performers' and authors' royalty rights), the Consumer Protection Act and CCPA guidelines on misleading ads and endorsements, ASCI codes and influencer guidelines, and any federation or broadcaster rules supplied. Record versions and dates.
2. Map the rights chain. Who owns what today? What must be assigned or licensed? Copyright assignment must be in writing; check the statutory defaults on term and territory if the deed is silent, and the bar on assigning royalty rights in underlying works for some uses.
3. Draft the operative clauses by deal type:
   - **Production and talent**: services, credits, work-for-hire or assignment, moral rights waiver limits, performer consent, schedules.
   - **Music and sync**: composition and sound recording licences separately, collecting-society licences, cue sheets.
   - **OTT and publishing**: licensed rights, holdbacks, windows, content codes compliance, takedown.
   - **Endorsement and sponsorship**: deliverables, approvals, image and name use, disclosure duties, due diligence on claims, exclusivity, morality clause, federation clearance.
4. Add common terms: payment and withholding tax, warranties, indemnities, termination, effect on materials, data, confidentiality, dispute resolution.
5. Check consumer law risk for the endorser: claims made must be verified, and the endorser can be exposed for misleading endorsements.
6. Search for and read current authority on any disputed point (for example personality rights). Tag anything not read in full `[verify]`.

## Output

1. The draft agreement or a marked-up review with a clause-by-clause issue table.
2. A rights-chain checklist.
3. A short note of points to negotiate and risks.

## Guardrails and hand-offs

- Never draft an endorsement that hides the paid relationship.
- Never state stamp duty from memory; hand off to `state-stamp-duty-calculator`.
- For assignments and licences of IP standing alone, hand off to `ip-assignment-licence-drafter`.
- For ad copy and claims, hand off to `advertising-claims-reviewer`.
- Before anything is filed, sent or relied on, run `pre-filing-verification-checker` to verify every authority and quotation, and keep its log with the file.
